# Showcase entry

Owner: **Toby**. A showcase piece is both site content and career evidence, so build it well and
route it back to franzy-os.

## Build the entry
1. Read the existing `showcase/` entries and `images/` conventions; match the established shape.
2. Draft the copy: what the project is, franzy's role, the outcome, and the tech. Keep it clean,
   concrete, and honest; no em dashes. Do not invent scope, metrics, or credentials.
3. Add or optimize images; keep the site fast (right-sized assets).
4. Wire the entry into the site's showcase index/navigation.

## Verify
5. Preview it with the `site-preview` skill: build, serve, and check the rendered entry in the
   browser at normal and narrow widths.

## Ship and capture
6. Pushing or deploying to the live site is confirmation-required: show the preview, then wait.
7. A new portfolio piece is franzy-os-worthy and usually relates to `career/`. Drop a capture
   file at `/srv/files/projects/franzy-os/inbox/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` so Kiki can link it into
   his career narrative and story bank.
